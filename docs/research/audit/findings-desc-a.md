## Bonus

**Probable duplicates in the dataset (merge or cross-link):**
- `blakhiyya-archaeological-site` = `anthedon-harbour` — same site (Tell Iblakhiyeh/Blakhiyah IS Anthedon), listed with different destruction dates.
- `ibn-uthman-mosque` = `ibn-othman-mosque` — same Shuja'iyya mosque, two spellings, conflicting dates (2024-07-15 vs 2024-07-01).
- `subat-al-alami` = `raghib-al-alami-house` — the latter's own text says "Also referred to as 'Subat al-Alami'".
- `napoleon-fort` (Al-Muntar Hill) = `tell-al-muntar` — same hill/tell.
- `al-katib-mosque` = likely `katib-wilaya-mosque` (no separate 13th-c. "Al-Katib Mosque" attested).
- `akkad-museum` possibly = `al-qarara-cultural-museum` (no independent "Akkad Museum" found in coverage of the Khan Younis/Qarara museum — verify before keeping both).

**Enrichment found while checking:** Al Qarara Cultural Museum was founded 2016 by Mohamed & Najla Abu Lahia, won the 2022 ICCROM-Sharjah Award; ~1,000 of 3,500 artifacts recovered from rubble in 2025 — stronger, sourceable detail than the current vague "20th century" text.

**Notable missing sites encountered:** Aybaki Mosque (Mamluk), Sheikh Khaled Mosque, Blakhiya Byzantine cemetery (distinct from the harbour), Othman bin Qashqar Mosque, Deir el-Belah CWGC War Cemetery (distinct from the Gaza War Cemetery), Gaza Baptist Church, Zawiyat Ibn Arraq.
